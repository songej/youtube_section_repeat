Repeat any part of a YouTube video from your keyboard.

Press S where a passage starts and E where it ends, then R to loop it. Mark as many sections as you like and step through them with A and D.

Add to Chrome Free on the Chrome Web Store

S E R

Shortcuts

S
Start a section
E
End it. Press again later to add the next section from where the last one ended.
R
Turn repeat on or off
A D
Go to the previous or next section
1 – 9
Jump to a section by its number
X
Delete the section that’s playing
Z
Undo the last delete
Q Q
Press twice to clear every section on this video
W
Leave the playlist and stay on this video (Focus Mode)

Letters are shown for a QWERTY keyboard. On other layouts the shortcuts stay on the same keys, and the extension shows each one the way your keyboard labels it.

Good to know

  • Works on YouTube video pages in Chrome 102 or later. Shorts and live streams aren’t supported.
  • Sections are saved for each video and come back when you open it again.
  • Open the extension from Chrome’s toolbar to see the sections on the video you’re watching, jump to one or delete it, and to export or import your sections as a file.
  • In an incognito window, sections last only until you leave the page.
  • The extension is available in 25 languages.

Privacy

Your sections are stored in your browser and never sent anywhere. There’s no account and no analytics, and the extension runs only on YouTube. Read the privacy policy.
